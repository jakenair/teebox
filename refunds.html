Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Summary

If an item never arrives or is materially not as described, open a dispute from the order within 7 days of delivery. The seller and TeeBox are notified, and we work it out with you. Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your card-network rights are unaffected.

1. What a Dispute Covers

You can open a dispute when:

  • The item never arrived. Tracking never shows delivery, or the seller never shipped.
  • The item is materially not as described. Wrong model, undisclosed major damage, a counterfeit, or condition well below the listed grade.
  • The item arrived damaged.

2. What a Dispute Doesn't Cover

  • Buyer's remorse. You changed your mind, found a better price, or no longer want the item.
  • Minor condition differences within the listed grade. Used gear varies; the condition rating on the listing is the baseline.
  • Sizing on accurately listed apparel. If the listing states the size and the item matches it, fit is not a defect.
  • Purchases made outside TeeBox. If you paid a seller outside the app, TeeBox cannot help with that transaction.

3. How to Open a Dispute

  1. Open the order from your Orders tab in the TeeBox app or website.
  2. Tap Report a problem, pick the reason, and describe what happened. Photos and tracking details help.
  3. Do this within 7 days of delivery. Disputes opened later are reviewed only in exceptional circumstances.

4. What Happens Next

  • The seller and TeeBox are notified by email as soon as you submit.
  • We work it out with you and the seller over email. We may ask either of you for photos, tracking, or your message history.
  • The outcome depends on the facts: a full or partial refund, a return followed by a refund, or no refund where the item matches the listing.
  • If the item needs to go back to the seller, we confirm the return address with you first. Ship it tracked and keep your proof of postage. Don't return an item to an address TeeBox hasn't confirmed.

TeeBox is the venue, not the seller, so we can't promise a particular outcome in advance. We do review every dispute and reply to every one.

5. Refund Method and Timing

Refunds are returned to your original payment method through Stripe. Once a refund is issued, Stripe and your bank typically take 5–10 business days to post it. TeeBox does not control how quickly your bank credits the refund.

6. Your Card-Network Rights

Opening a TeeBox dispute does not limit any rights you have with your card issuer or under applicable law. We ask that you give the in-app dispute a chance first, because it is usually faster. If you file a chargeback, the card network's process runs alongside ours and controls the funds, and we keep working with you either way.

7. In-App Purchases (Pro Seller Subscription)

Refunds for the Pro Seller subscription purchased through the Apple App Store are handled by Apple under its own refund policy. Submit a refund request to Apple at https://support.apple.com/HT204084. TeeBox cannot process Apple-billed refunds.

Refunds for the Pro Seller subscription purchased on the web through Stripe are handled by TeeBox at its discretion. Email support@teeboxmarket.com within 14 days of the charge.

8. Contact

Dispute questions or escalations: support@teeboxmarket.com.
