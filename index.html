Seller Terms of Service

1. Seller Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to sell on TeeBox. By agreeing to these terms you confirm that you have full legal ownership of every item you list and the right to sell it.

2. TeeBox is a Venue, Not a Party

TeeBox provides a platform that connects buyers and sellers. TeeBox is not the seller of any item, does not take possession of items, and does not authenticate, inspect, or guarantee the condition or genuineness of items. The contract of sale is solely between the buyer and the seller.

3. Accurate Listings & Photos

You agree to provide accurate descriptions, correct condition ratings, and honest photos that show the actual item being sold. Misrepresentation, counterfeits, and stolen goods are prohibited and will result in immediate suspension and potential legal referral.

4. Shipping Obligations

Once a sale is confirmed, you agree to ship the item directly to the buyer within 3 business days using a tracked, insured carrier service. Failure to ship may result in refund to the buyer and account suspension.

5. Fees

TeeBox charges a seller transaction fee of 8.5% of the item price, deducted automatically from your payout. Shipping is paid by the buyer at checkout and funds the prepaid shipping label — nothing is deducted from your payout for shipping. Use the TeeBox label: if you ship on your own instead, the buyer's shipping payment is not passed through to you. The fee may change with 30 days' notice.

6. Payouts

Payouts are issued to your linked bank account on Stripe's standard payout schedule after a successful sale. Buyers have 7 days from delivery to open a dispute; a confirmed dispute may reverse a payout.

7. Disputes & Indemnification

You agree that TeeBox is not liable for any loss or damages arising from a transaction. You will indemnify TeeBox against any claim, fee, or expense related to your listings or conduct on the platform. Disputes between buyers and sellers must first attempt resolution through the in-app messaging.

8. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. Any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts of Cook County, Illinois.

9. Prohibited Items

No counterfeit or replica clubs, shafts, putters, or balls. No stolen merchandise. No tour-only or non-conforming equipment misrepresented as USGA-conforming. No range balls or driving-range pickup balls. No items obtained via warranty fraud or as tournament samples not authorized for resale. No used gloves, hats, or items with bodily-fluid contamination. No firearms, ammunition, or weapons even if golf-adjacent. Violation results in immediate listing removal and account suspension.

10. Intellectual Property Representations

You represent that every item listed is genuine, not counterfeit, and that your listing photos and descriptions do not infringe any third party's copyright, trademark, or right of publicity. You indemnify TeeBox against all IP claims arising from your listings.

11. Stripe Connected Account Agreement

By becoming a seller, you separately agree to the Stripe Services Agreement and the Stripe Connected Account Agreement. TeeBox is not party to your relationship with Stripe.

12. Tax Reporting

Stripe will issue Form 1099-K to sellers who exceed federal IRS reporting thresholds. You consent to Stripe collecting your TIN/SSN and reporting your earnings. You are solely responsible for the tax treatment of your sales.

13. Chargebacks

If a buyer initiates a chargeback that is decided in the buyer's favor by the card network, the chargeback amount plus any network fee will be deducted from your payout balance or future payouts. TeeBox does not guarantee the result of any chargeback dispute.

14. Account Suspension and Appeal

TeeBox may suspend a seller for confirmed counterfeit listings, failure to ship two or more sold orders, dispute findings against the seller, repeated policy violations, or DMCA/counterfeit takedowns. We will notify you by email and you may appeal within 14 days by emailing support@teeboxmarket.com. Our decision on appeal is final.

15. Off-Platform Circumvention

Soliciting or completing a sale outside TeeBox after a buyer has contacted you through the platform is prohibited. First violation: warning. Second: permanent suspension and forfeiture of pending payouts.

About TeeBox

The peer-to-peer marketplace for golfers.

TeeBox is a place to buy and sell golf gear directly between members. We focus on the categories you actually care about: clubs, apparel, headcovers, accessories, balls, and bags, with real peer-to-peer pricing and a flat 8.5% seller fee.

How it works

  1. List in seconds. Agree to the seller terms and post photos with a price. No application, no waiting.
  2. Message before you buy. Every listing has a built-in chat with the seller so you can ask about condition, sizing, or shipping before you commit.
  3. Pay with confidence. Checkout runs through Stripe with Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Card details never touch our servers.
  4. Sellers ship directly. Once a sale is confirmed, the seller has 3 business days to ship with tracking. Payments are processed securely through Stripe. If something goes wrong, open a dispute from your order within 7 days of delivery and we work it out with you and the seller.
  5. Low fee. Sellers pay 8.5% on completed sales. No listing fees, no buyer fees.

What TeeBox is not

TeeBox is a venue, like eBay or Craigslist. We do not own the items, do not authenticate them, and we are not party to the transaction between buyer and seller. Read the Terms of Service for the full picture before you list or purchase.
