How shipping works

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

The buyer pays for shipping at checkout. The seller gets a prepaid label, ships within 3 business days, and tracking shows on the order. Nothing is taken from the seller's payout for the label.

1. What shipping costs

ItemShipping
Apparel, headcovers$10
Accessories, balls$12
Shoes$14
Clubs, bagsThe carrier's rate to your address. Enter your ZIP on the listing for an estimate; checkout shows the exact price.

Checkout and your receipt list the item, shipping, protection and total on separate lines.

2. Shipping protection

Items over $100 ship with protection, paid by the buyer: 1.25% of the item price. It covers the package up to the item price while the carrier has it. If a protected package is lost or damaged on the way, report a problem on the order so we can look into a claim.

3. Pick the box (clubs)

When you list a club you choose what's in the box. The buyer's price and your label are for that box.

What's in the boxBox
Single club or small bundle (1-3 clubs)48 × 8 × 8 in, up to 7 lb
Partial set (4-7 clubs)48 × 10 × 8 in, up to 12 lb
Full set or oversized (8+ clubs)48 × 14 × 10 in, up to 22 lb
Bag38 × 14 × 12 in, up to 12 lb

Measure the packed box before you drop it off. A box that is bigger or heavier than your pick costs more to ship.

When a box is bigger or heavier than declared: our policy on carrier adjustments is coming soon. Today nothing is deducted from sellers for them.

4. How to pack it

Cover it, pad it, and tape every seam.

5. Ship by the date on your order

  • You have 3 business days after the sale. Weekends and postal holidays don't count. The exact date is on your Sold tab and in the sold email.
  • We remind you on day 2 and day 3.
  • Print the prepaid label from your Sold tab. For smaller items you can get a QR code instead and USPS prints the label at the counter.
  • USPS labels can be picked up from your door: schedule a free USPS pickup.

6. Tracking

With a TeeBox label, the order page follows the carrier's scans: label created, in transit, out for delivery, delivered. Buyers get an email when it ships and when it is delivered. If you use your own label, enter the tracking number and the order links to the carrier.

7. What the seller is paid

The sale price minus TeeBox's fee. The exact amount is on your Sold tab and in the sold email. Shipping and protection are paid by the buyer and are not part of your payout. Payouts follow Stripe's schedule.

8. Something went wrong?

Open the order and tap Report a problem within 7 days of delivery. See the Refund and Returns Policy, or write to support@teeboxmarket.com.
