Rodeo Dunes: Colorado’s Next Great Golf Destination

TeeBox · September 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Every so often a piece of ground turns up that’s so perfect for golf it feels like it was waiting the whole time. Rodeo Dunes — a Bill Coore and Ben Crenshaw design on 4,000 acres of towering sand in Roggen, Colorado — is that kind of ground. It opened to national acclaim, it’s already spoken of in the same breath as Sand Hills and Bandon, and it comes with a twist no other course can claim: an actual rodeo. Here’s the story, in seven pictures.

1. The land came first
The Site · 4,000 acres · Roggen, Colorado

Great courses start with great dirt, and this is some of the best in America. The property is the Cervi family ranch — cattle and rodeo country worked by the same family since 1882, more than 140 years, and never for sale until now. Under the grass sat 4,000 acres of natural sand dunes rolling to the horizon: the rarest raw material in golf, and the reason Sand Hills, Bandon, and now Rodeo Dunes exist at all.

2. Closer than it looks
Getting There · ~45 min from Denver · off I-76

For all the big-sky, middle-of-nowhere feel, it’s an easy trip. Rodeo Dunes sits in Roggen, Colorado, straight up I-76 — about 45 minutes from Denver and roughly 42 miles from Denver International Airport. Fly in, drive out onto the plains, and the dunes appear right where you’d least expect them.

3. The Bandon Dunes family
The Owners · Dream Golf · The Keiser name

If the pedigree feels familiar, it should. Rodeo Dunes is a Dream Golf project — the company run by Michael and Chris Keiser, sons of Mike Keiser, the man who built Bandon Dunes and reshaped American golf. The family portfolio reads like a bucket list: Bandon, Sand Valley, Cabot, Lido. Rodeo Dunes is the newest name on it — and the family’s first big statement out on the high plains.

4. There’s an actual rodeo
The Twist · Cervi Championship Rodeo · Duel at the Dunes

Here’s what puts this place in a category of one: there’s a real rodeo. The Cervi family runs Cervi Championship Rodeo, one of the biggest PRCA stock contractors in the country, and they staged the first-ever Duel at the Dunes — 20 of the best roughstock cowboys riding saddle bronc, bareback, and bulls, in an arena set on the sand between the first and 18th holes. Golf and the American West, on the same ground.

5. Six courses on the board
What’s Coming · Up to six courses · A short course, a putting green & a lodge

And the Coore-Crenshaw course is only the opening act. The masterplan for these 4,000 acres runs to as many as six courses. Next up is a second 18 from longtime Coore & Crenshaw associate Jim Craig, joined over time by a short course, an enormous putting green, and a lodge — the full destination-resort blueprint the Keisers wrote at Bandon and Sand Valley.

6. Founders now, the rest of us in 2027
How to Play · Members in 2026 · Public play 2027

For now, Rodeo Dunes belongs to its founders — the members who bought in early to be first through the gates in 2026. Public play opens in 2027, and if the preview season is any sign, tee times won’t sit around: the first ones went in days. Colorado’s next great golf destination is here. The only question left is when you get your turn.

Golf and the American West on the same 4,000 acres — with a real rodeo running between the first and 18th holes.
